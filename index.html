About

I am a disciplined and motivated individual with a strong academic record. I completed my B.Sc. (Engg.) in Industrial and Production Engineering at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST) in 2026, graduating first in my class with distinction (CGPA 3.91/4.00). My thesis was supervised by Prof. Abul Mukid Md. Mukaddes, under whose guidance I continue to work on computational bioheat transfer, using finite element thermal modelling and simulation. I am also working with Prof. Choudhury Abul Anam Rashed on graph neural networks for forecasting pharmaceutical shortages, and with Prof. Mohammad Iqbal on ergonomics and human–technology interaction.

I have proven experience in research, communication and organisational leadership, and I bring a productive attitude to all of it. I am known for reliability, clarity in execution, and a thoughtful approach to problem-solving. I am committed to contributing meaningfully through precision, adaptability, and a results-driven mindset.
